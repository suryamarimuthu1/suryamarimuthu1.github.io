Hi! I'm Surya, and I'm a Lab Manager at the Psychology Department at Yale University, working with [Dr.Julia Leonard](https://www.learninglab.yale.edu/). Before joining Yale, I received my BS in Cognitive Science and BA in Psychology in 2025 from UC Santa Cruz, where I worked with [Dr. Maureen Callanan](https://campusdirectory.ucsc.edu/detail.php?uid=callanan). I also worked in [Dr. Hyowon Gweon's lab](https://sll.stanford.edu/index.html) at Stanford as an undergraduate summer research assistant. 

I'm broadly interested in how young children decide what and how to learn. In a world filled with more information than we could ever acquire, how do children -- who have relatively little knowledge of what is worth learning and how much effort to invest -- make complex learning and effort-allocation decisions? 

In my free time, you can find me playing and watching soccer, basketball (Go Dubs), and football. I also enjoy cooking South Indian food for my friends and watching them suffer as they experience real spice for the first time.
