Hi! I'm Surya, and I'm a Lab Manager at the Psychology Department in Yale University, working with Dr. Julia Leonard. I received my B.S. in Cognitive Science and B.A. in Psychology in 2025 from UC Santa Cruz, where I worked with Dr. Maureen Callanan studying how children learn from interactions their caregivers. As a summer intern, I also worked in Dr. Hyowon Gweon's lab at Stanford studying children's curiosity about the self. 

======
I'm interested in how young children decide what and how to learn. In a world filled with more information than we could ever acquire, how do children -- who have relatively little knowledge of what is worth learning and how much effort to invest -- make complex learning and effort-allocation decisions?
